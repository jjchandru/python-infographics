UNIT 2.2 - LOOPS

(10)

range(start, stop) — counts from start up to stop, exclusive.

SYNTAX - RANGE(START, STOP)

for {loop_variable} in range({start}, {stop}):
    {statement(s)}
{start}
lower bound, inclusive
{stop}
upper bound, exclusive

EXAMPLE

for i in range(2, 6):
    print(i)

Output:
2
3
4
5
