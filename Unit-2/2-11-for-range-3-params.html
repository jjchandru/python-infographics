UNIT 2.2 - LOOPS

(11)

range(start, stop, step) — counts from start to stop, exclusive, by step.

SYNTAX - RANGE(START, STOP, STEP)

for {loop_variable} in range({start}, {stop}, {step}):
    {statement(s)}
{start}
lower bound, inclusive
{stop}
upper bound, exclusive
{step}
amount to add each time — negative counts down

EXAMPLE

for i in range(10, 0, -2):
    print(i)

Output:
10
8
6
4
2
