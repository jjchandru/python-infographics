4 ways to write a for loop — pick based on what you need from the data.

4 WAYS TO USE FOR

1. Sequence
iterate a string, list, tuple, set, or dictionary
2. Range
loop through numbers defined by starting number, ending number, and step size
3. Enumerate
get index and value together
4. Zip
pair elements from two sequences together
