3 ways to call range() — pick based on how many boundaries you need.

3 WAYS TO USE RANGE()

1. range(stop)
numbers from 0 up to stop, step size 1
2. range(start, stop)
numbers from start up to stop, step size 1
3. range(start, stop, step)
numbers from start to stop, moving by step
