UNIT 3.4 - DICTIONARIES

(6)

Deleting a key-value pair with del

KEY EXISTS

student = {"name": "Ravi", "age": 20, "dept": "CSE"}

del student["dept"]
print(student)

Output:
{'name': 'Ravi', 'age': 20}

KEY MISSING

student = {"name": "Ravi", "age": 20, "dept": "CSE"}

del student["year"]

Output:
KeyError: 'year'

EXPLANATION

  • del dict_name[key] removes the pair entirely — nothing is returned.
  • If the key does not exist, del raises a KeyError.
  • del is a statement, not a method — no parentheses, no dot before it.
