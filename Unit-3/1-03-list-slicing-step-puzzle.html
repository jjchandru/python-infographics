UNIT 3.1 - LISTS

(3)

Slicing a list with start, stop, and step

🧩 PUZZLE - PREDICT THE OUTPUT

temps = [30, 32, 29, 35, 31, 28, 33]

print(temps[1:4])
print(temps[::2])
print(temps[::-1])
print(temps[1:6:2])

HINTS

  • Square brackets with colons represent slicing.
  • Slicing cuts and extracts portions of a list.
  • The three options within square brackets represent start, stop (non-inclusive), and step.
