UNIT 3.2 - TUPLES

(5)

Nested tuples — a tuple whose items are themselves tuples

EXAMPLE

# x, y coords of a triangle's points
triangle = ((0, 0), (4, 0), (2, 3))

print(triangle)
print(triangle[0])
print(triangle[1][0])

OUTPUT

((0, 0), (4, 0), (2, 3))
(0, 0)
4

EXPLANATION

  • triangle is a tuple of three (x, y) points — each point is itself a tuple.
  • triangle[0] reaches one inner tuple, the first corner point (0, 0).
  • triangle[1][0] chains two indexes — the second point, then its x-coordinate, 4.
