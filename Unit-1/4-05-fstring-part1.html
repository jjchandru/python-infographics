UNIT 1.4 - STATEMENTS, CONVERSION & OUTPUT

(5)

Helps formatting without concatenation.

SYNTAX - FORMATTED STRING LITERAL

f"[[<chars>] {<field>}]*"
[]
optional
*
can be repeated
<chars>
Characters of String
<field>
{identifier | literal | expression}

EXAMPLE

name = "Alex"

age = 15

print(f"{name} is {age} years old.")

Output:
Alex is 15 years old.
