UNIT 1.3 - OPERATORS AND EXPRESSIONS

(1)

ARITHMETIC OPERATORS

+, -, *, /, //, %, **

SYNTAX - ARITHMETIC EXPRESSION

{operand} {arithmetic-operator} {operand}
{operand}
{identifier | literal}

NOTE: The expression result is not stored or printed on its own – assign it to a variable or print it.

{identifier} = {arithmetic-expression}
print({arithmetic-expression})

EXAMPLE

age = 5          # no output
age = age + 10
print(age)
