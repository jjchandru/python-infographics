UNIT 1.4 - STATEMENTS, CONVERSION & OUTPUT

(6)

Control how a value is displayed inside a field.

SYNTAX - FORMAT SPECIFIER

{<field>:<format-specifier>}
<format-specifier>
refer examples below

EXAMPLES

ExampleOutputDescription
f"{3.14159:.2f}" 3.14 Rounds to 2 decimals
f"{1234567:,}" 1,234,567 Comma thousands separator
f"{42:>6}"     42 Right-aligned, width 6
f"{42:<6}" 42     Left-aligned, width 6
