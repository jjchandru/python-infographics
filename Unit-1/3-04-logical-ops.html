UNIT 1.3 - OPERATORS AND EXPRESSIONS

(4)

LOGICAL OPERATORS

and, or, not

SYNTAX - LOGICAL EXPRESSION

{operand} and|or {operand}
not {operand}
{operand}
{identifier | literal | arithmetic-expression}

EXAMPLE

age = 5
is_minor = age >= 4 and age <= 18    # True
is_not_minor = not is_minor          # False

LOGICAL OPERATOR PRECEDENCE

(), not, and, or
