UNIT 1.3 - OPERATORS AND EXPRESSIONS

(3)

COMPARISON OPERATORS

==, !=, <, >, <=, >=

SYNTAX - COMPARISON EXPRESSION

{operand} {comparison-operator} {operand}
{operand}
{identifier | literal}

NOTE: The result is a bool (True/False) and is not stored or printed on its own – assign it to a variable or print it.

{identifier} = {comparison-expression}
print({comparison-expression})

EXAMPLE

age = 5
is_adult = age >= 18
print(is_adult)      # prints False
